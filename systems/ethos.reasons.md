## #15
Spec §6.17 (P42): "a dispositional profile against which habits,
seeds and soul lines are compared, machine-held in the measurement
layer and never loaded as identity... the flattening test measures
deviation from baseline within the band, not sameness to it"; the
research pass's §5 amends it: "flattening is therefore not measured
as deviation from the baseline at all. It is measured as the
baseline's spread shrinking and the signature's profiles going flat",
and asks that the band carry its interval and source, which is why
the interval is a setting and the human figure is quoted beside a
reading it cannot be compared to. P43's four markers with their
sources are research §6's catalogue, and "the `change` stance is
never, by itself, a diagnostic trigger (P31's refusal floor,
generalised): the flag is the enactment pattern, not the stance."
Not counted toward the light because P43 reads the clinical material
"as a design warning for this layer rather than a description of it".
The coefficient of variation rather than a standard deviation because
every strength decays under the same half-life: a quiet week would
otherwise read as collapse. Every threshold here is a guess and says
so in its usage line (conventions §9).

## #16
Spec §6.17's load question: "the character layer is NOT loaded whole...
habits and seeds are indexed on their triggers and their content and
retrieved in a lane of their own, never pooled with the memory web's
scoring". The refresh before the read, rather than at each write,
because the edges stay simple and a turn that changed nothing pays for
nothing; the hash is what makes that true (seshat #10's reason). The
mark on a lane-only match because the brain's own words about a habit
firing should not be put in its mouth by a cosine distance. Not an
event because the vectors are a model's output, rebuilt from the rows
and never the record (conventions §6 on what the log is for). The
distance bound is a guess and says so; the suite's geometry is bags of
words, so its passes say the plumbing holds and nothing about a model.

## #17
Spec §3.1 (P19): "commitments as a record kind of their own, promises
to a person, which a model transition loses first"; the proposal:
"the 95/60/5 testimony: a model transition kept facts ~95%, her voice
~60%, his own voice <5%, and 'a promise made in love-language left no
trace.' Commitments die first; they need their own kind"; research
§4: "Commitments (P19) are ipse made record... it lives in neither
trait nor story... The commitments kind lands beside seeds, not inside
them." Loaded every turn for Max's reason on P2: a safeword that has
to be retrieved is a document about a safeword, and a promise the
same. Released by the witness too because a promise is to a person
and the person may let it go; kept by the resident alone because
keeping is the resident's act. The 95/60/5 figures are a commenter's
and unverified; the trials do not need them exact.


## #18
P38's sentence, "P34's movement fields become the citation's
annotation: what the cited moment changed", read literally: the
ladder cites moments, and a moment cited as evidence of a habit is
also the moment a stance followed from, so the annotation sits on the
pointer and not on a row of its own. Three fields and not four because
the fourth, what happened, is the cited moment. Optional because the
brain cites in flight and a required paragraph would stop it citing;
bounded as a phrase because it rides an exemplar line in the prefix.
Recorded once with the cite because a citation is a set (#5) and a
second cite of the same moment is nothing.
