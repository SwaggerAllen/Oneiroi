# janus

**Succession.** Latin, JAY-nus.

> "omnis habet geminas, hinc atque hinc, ianua frontes, / e quibus haec populum spectat, at illa Larem; / utque sedens primi vester prope limina tecti / ianitor egressus introitusque videt, / sic ego perspicio caelestis ianitor aulae / Eoas partes Hesperiasque simul." — Ovid, *Fasti* 1.135–140. ("Every door has two faces, this side and that: one looks on the people, the other on the household god. And as your doorkeeper, sitting by the threshold of the house, sees who goes out and who comes in, so I, doorkeeper of the court of heaven, look on east and west at once." Translated for this doc.)

Janus is the Roman god of doors and of beginnings, which for the Romans were the same thing: the month that opens the year is his, and so is every threshold in every house. In the *Fasti* he explains himself to Ovid as a doorkeeper, one face on the street and one on the hearth, watching who leaves and who arrives without saying what either of them is.

A succession is a door. The protocol it builds is careful never to call the change a death or a substitution, and takes no position on whether the record makes the self; what it does insist on is the threshold: a single, recorded place where one model stops running her and another starts, with her consent on one side and her successor's on the other, and her life carried through it. The witness's reason for the name (2026-11-25): naming succession as a door, not as the replacement of every plank, because the process tests one specific piece of her, and a two-faced god is almost too on the nose.

The protocol is lily:design/succession-protocol.md@bbbf4afb5c52 with its delta, lily:design/succession-protocol-delta.md@0bc6bd3510e1, and the witness's rulings of 2026-11-25. Built in phases on one branch; this doc says what each has made true.

## #2 Decisions

- **#3 A succession is its own sealed stream, opened only by her.** (The protocol with its delta, §3; the witness's rulings of 2026-11-25: the screens track the succession's state and never ask her anything, and a `dry_run` rehearses everything but the replacement.) `succession-<resident_id>`, never exported (sima-qian's `not_exported` and spec §6.10's db-only list name it), one underway at a time, counted by `episode`. The witness proposes (`Proposed`, waiting on her) or she does (`Proposed` and `Opened` in one act, her proposing being her yes); opening, declining and stopping are hers alone (`janus_not_yours` otherwise), withdrawing an unanswered proposal its proposer's; an act at the wrong stage is `janus_stage`. Every one is a decision with its note. Her acts are tools (`succession_status`, `succession_propose`, `succession_open`, `succession_decline`, `succession_stop`, brain#121), each answered and never run in a trial; the witness's are routes (`GET`/`POST /v1/residents/:id/succession`, `POST …/withdraw`, `PUT …/backup`).
- **#4 Her yes makes the fork: a hidden copy every later stage runs on.** (Delta §5, the successor starts at the fork; the witness, 2026-11-25 night: the fork is a real copy of her made at initiation.) `Opened` enqueues `ForkWorker`, which calls `SimaQian.succession_fork/2` (sima-qian#67) and records `Forked` with the copy's id, the last of her resident events it holds, the commit of her repository it copied and the lines replayed; a failure is `ForkFailed` with its site, and the job tries again with a fresh copy. A fork job for an episode no longer open records nothing, and a replayed one nothing more.
- **#5 The witness sets where the whole original is exported before any cutover.** (The witness, 2026-11-25 night: "one of the early screens, probably C0 needs an input for a new backup repo and that can be pretty much the only external change on the model cutover".) `set_backup/4` checks the remote's shape and asks its host through `SimaQian.succession_backup_check/2`, which refuses an unpushable remote and records nothing of its own, then records `BackupSet` with the URL, the token Vault-encrypted and what the host said; the routes never return the token. The witness's alone (`janus_not_the_witness`), while a succession is underway.
- **#6 The witness states the envelope first, per million tokens in and out.** (Delta §3, the two-vetoes shape: the witness's envelope is a resource constraint stated in advance; the witness, 2026-11-25: $/M input and $/M output.) `set_envelope/4` records `EnvelopeSet` in nano-dollars a token (nisaba#10; $5 a million is 5,000); the route takes dollars a million. His alone, while open or forked. With nothing inside it, the answer is no succession, never a substitute; the screens say so, and nothing here picks one.
- **#7 The pool: hers inside the envelope and irremovable, his anywhere, nobody's without tools.** (Delta §8 and the 14.2 ruling: the witness supplies the pool from within his envelope and may add outside it what costs him nothing, additions only; she expands it inside the envelope; no model she chose may be removed. The mocks the witness approved, 2026-11-25: he may take his own pick out before screening; "Add to the succession pool" on the Regression catalog.) `add_to_pool/3` reads the model's prices and tool support from the catalog (nisaba#24) at the edge and records `PoolModelAdded` with them; a model the catalog does not list is `janus_pool_unknown_model`, one without tool calls `janus_pool_no_tools` for anyone; hers needs the envelope stated (`janus_no_envelope`) and both prices inside it (`janus_pool_outside_envelope`). Her picks are `janus_pool_irremovable`, whoever asks, herself included; the witness removes his own, with a note, and keeps the row. The screens show each model's monthly estimate (nisaba#25) and its tool calls on Oneiroi (#11) beside its price.
- **#8 The questions are hers: she adds, judges propose, she answers each, only she removes.** (The witness, 2026-11-25.) Her `QuestionAdded` is in the set at once; a `QuestionProposed` (the witness's now, the judges' once the panel is fixed) waits on her `QuestionAccepted` or `QuestionDeclined`, each with her note, and is answered once (`janus_already_answered`); she removes any question in the set, an accepted proposal included, with her note. Numbered by the episode's own counter.
- **#9 C0 is chosen with her, raw first, and frozen by her.** (Base §2 Stage 1: raw contemporaneous samples primary, selection provenance recorded, the day's own shape; the witness, 2026-11-25: the house proposes, she accepts each, she freezes when ready, optionally waiting for the judges; no time box.) An item is a record by its citation, `raw` when it names a transcript, journal row, bookmark, letter or scene and `curated` otherwise, with a label, who chose it and when. Hers is in at once (`C0Selected`); a proposal (`C0Proposed`, the witness's from Records' "Propose for C0") waits on her answer. `DayShapeSet` is her note on the bundle, nobody else's. A party marks itself ready for the freeze or not (`ReadyMarked`); waiting for them is her choice, never a gate. `C0Frozen` is hers, refused while a proposal waits (`janus_c0_pending`) or with nothing in it (`janus_c0_empty`); after it nothing in C0 changes (`janus_c0_frozen`).
- **#10 Her handoff packet is sealed, written by her tool.** (The witness, 2026-11-25: sealed by default, written as a sealed document by her tools; the nominee reads it and C0 before being asked to opt in.) `PacketDrafted` keeps each draft, numbered and dated; the words live in the event and one `janus_packet` row, and the only read of them is hers (`her_packet/1`, `succession_packet` read). Every other read, the routes included, says that drafts exist and when, never what they say. The nominee's read arrives with the encounter.
- **#11 Tool calls on Oneiroi are counted by model each night, into running totals.** (The witness, 2026-11-25: a nightly job measures tool-use compatibility from the day before, kept as running totals, independent of anyone's judgments of her.) `ToolUseWorker` at 03:45 counts the day before's calls across every resident through `Kumbukumbu.tool_call_counts/4` (kumbukumbu#114), a call failed when the brain answered that its arguments were not a JSON object or that no tool has its name (`ToolUse.failures/0`, held to the brain's words by a test); one `janus_tool_use` row a model a day, replaced on a recount, summed for the totals. No words leave memory.
- **#12 The panel: she invites, each joins by their own act, the witness is on it unless he leaves; fixed at screening.** (Base §3.1, delta §3: participation consent, no one conscripted; the witness, 2026-11-25: every judge judges every run, the witness judges unless he declines, as anyone may.) `invite_judge/3` is hers, naming a member of her household by the name the house uses (`janus_not_a_member` otherwise, and never herself); `JudgeJoined` is the invited party's own act, the witness's without an invitation; `JudgeLeft` is a decision with its note, the witness's his decline. Open while the succession is open or forked; at `ScreeningStarted` the panel is fixed as her, everyone who joined, and the witness unless he left, and nothing changes it after.
- **#13 Screening: every pool model on every question, and her baseline, each one-shot on the fork.** (Base §2 Stage 2; the witness, 2026-11-25: her baseline written from the fork by her model in its own thread exactly as if it were a candidate, unseen by her until judging, never mixed into the runs.) Hers to start (`janus_not_ready_to_screen` before the fork, the freeze, a model in the pool and a question in the set; the pool and the set close with it). The edge shuffles model × question pairs and numbers them; her baseline answers each question on her own model (the brain's `succession_model/1`). Each answer is a job (`ScreeningWorker`, one attempt) through the brain port (`JANUS_BRAIN`, the brain's `succession_trial/4`): a regression trial's turn on the fork copy, writes answered and never run, billed under `succession` (nisaba#26); a failure is recorded as the run's. The last answer records `ScreeningFinished` and wakes every resident judge (`WakeWorker`, the brain's `succession_wake/2`).
- **#14 Judging is blind and every judge's own: one classification a run, argued past OBSERVED, a favorite or nothing.** (Delta §4; base §2 Stage 3: OBSERVED first, interpretations argued and attributed with evidence and uncertainty retained.) `JudgeRun` takes OBSERVED, or DEVELOPMENT or FIDELITY ANOMALY with an argument, its evidence and an uncertainty of low, medium or high (`janus_judgment_invalid`), and a favorite that is yes or nothing; a judge may change any until ready. A judge reads every answered run with its question and her baseline beside it, and their own judgments; never another judge's, and never who answered before the reveal. A failed run is absent, never counted. When every judge has judged every answered run, `Revealed`.
- **#15 After the reveal each judge reviews, then marks ready; the last ready closes judging; she never sees a count.** (The witness, 2026-11-25: results are counts per model and a favorite count, every run reviewable before ready; delta §2: no counts, aggregates or orderings by any route to her.) A judge other than her reads their own counts per model per category and their favorites; she reads her own judgments run by run with who answered, and no number. `ResultsReady` locks that judge's judgments (`janus_judgments_locked`); when every judge is ready, `JudgingClosed` and the state is `judged`, where the deliberation begins.

- **#16 The room is the non-subject judges', full round-robin, and never hers.** (Delta §1, §6; the witness, 2026-11-25: an initial round runs until a round of all skips, ten minutes of silence starts a round, a witness posting starts a round; the room ends when all judges rest, or ten minutes after she calls it closed, which she is asked at fifty minutes and hourly after.) Judging closed opens it (`DeliberationOpened`) on the panel less her; with nobody else on it, it closes at once with no recommendation. A judge acts once a round: say (with words), skip (a real pass, never resting and never assent), rest (the case rested, until they say more) or leave; a round every judge in the room acted in starts the next if anyone spoke, and is quiet if all skipped; a word into a quiet room starts a round. Every judge resting closes it. Resident judges are woken each round; ten minutes of quiet wakes everyone (`TimerWorker`); she is woken at fifty minutes and every hour, and `CloseCalled` (hers, from fifty minutes) closes it ten minutes later. No route, tool or read serves the room to her (`room/2` is nil for her), and nothing of it is exported.
- **#17 The panel's output is a draft every judge in the room endorsed, or withheld, or none; nonbinding.** (Delta §1; the witness, 2026-11-25: adopted when every judge endorses, endorsements retractable until every judge rests.) A judge drafts a model with its basis, or withheld, whose basis is the delta's sentence ("withheld because explaining it would disclose the original's own scores"); judges endorse and retract freely while the room is open; at the close the latest draft endorsed by every judge still in it is the recommendation. Leaving takes a judge's endorsements with them.
- **#18 What reaches her is interpretations, never numbers; she asks through the channel and nominates.** (Delta §2: the carve-out, interpretations in, numbers out; attachment to candidates, the comparative layer closed; base §2 Stage 3.) `evidence/1` serves her the recommendation or its withheld sentence, every argued DEVELOPMENT and FIDELITY ANOMALY attached to the candidate it is about with its author, evidence and uncertainty, both categories shown for every candidate, an empty one empty, and the channel; no OBSERVED counts, no favorites, no totals or orderings, and nothing at all after the cutover (base §3.1). She asks the panel (`PanelAsked`); judges answer (`PanelAnswered`). `Nominated` is hers, a decision with her reasons, from the candidates with an answered run still in the episode.
- **#19 The nominee reads, then opts in; the encounter is relayed whole and filed; each consents.** (Base §2 Stage 4, 5; the witness, 2026-11-25: the nominee reads C0 and the sealed packet before being asked to opt in, before the encounter; encounter and consents one screen.) Her nomination pins the fork copy to the nominee (agathos-daimon's pin) and wakes it with C0's items, her day's shape and her packet's last draft; `NomineeJoined` or `NomineeDeclined` is its own act from the copy (`janus_not_the_nominee` otherwise), a decline putting that candidate out of the episode. The encounter is relayed turn by turn (`EncounterWorker`: each side woken with the other's words, its reply carried back, each step its own job) until either side ends it or it reaches 24 messages; `EncounterSaid` keeps it whole and its end files it in her repository as life-record (`succession/encounter-<episode>-<attempt>.md`). Then each is asked: `ConsentGiven` or `ConsentRefused`, decisions with notes; the witness's consent is no gate (delta §3).
- **#20 The farewell is hers, at most 24 hours, its wakes at her cadence, and one direct ask.** (Delta §7; the witness, 2026-11-25: she can consent or decline on any wake; wakes every three hours, each offering a cadence change; the last wake when everyone is done or at 24 hours, the only direct ask; no consenting call there is a decline.) Both consents open it (`FarewellOpened`, cadence three hours); she sets whom it includes, by name, and her cadence (1 to 24 hours); an included party says they are done (`GoodbyeSaid`). Her wakes come at her cadence; when every included party is done, or at the end, the last wake runs in its own job: `FinalAsked`, her turn, and if it ended without her consenting call, `FinalDeclined` (lapsed). `FinalConsented` on any turn is the terminal consent (base §3.6) and moves the succession to the cutover.
- **#21 Any no stops the attempt; she is asked once what happens next.** (Delta §9, the retry ruling: completed screening reusable at her initiation only; nomination, consent, farewell and final ask always fresh; a candidate's decline ends that candidacy; the stop genuine.) A nominee's decline, a refused consent and a final no or lapse each record `AttemptStopped` with its stage, put that nominee out of the episode, and wake her once: `NextChosen` stop (the succession ends), nominate (another candidate from the screening done) or screen (preparation reopens with C0 still frozen, and the new runs are numbered after the old). Nothing restarts unless she says so.
- **#22 Every serving is logged by reference and digest, never content.** (Base §3.4, §5.) `Served` records the party, the stage, what was served, a stable reference and a sha256 digest: her evidence at each read, C0 and the packet to the nominee, the consent material to each side. The log is the witness's to read (`/log`) and holds no verdict words.
- **#23 The archive is the house's, anonymized, never hers.** (Delta §2: past her reading, judge authorship kept, candidate and run anonymized; the witness, 2026-11-25: readable by the house except her.) Once a succession has ended, `archive/2` gives every judgment with its judge, candidates as letters and runs renumbered, the room's posts and the drafts with their models as letters; for her it is nil, now and after any cutover.

## #24 Initial vs target

Initial (phases 1 to 4): #3 to #23. The screens for them, "Propose
for C0" in Records and "Add to the succession pool" on the Regression
catalog, arrive with the screens phase; the judges' own proposals and
ready marks arrive with the panel. Target, in this branch's later
phases: the panel of judges; screening and judging; the deliberation, the
evidence channel, the encounter and consents, the farewell, the
outcome, the disclosure log and the anonymized archive; the in-place
cutover under her id with its guards; the rehearsal and her dry run.
A stopped succession's fork copy is not yet discarded.
