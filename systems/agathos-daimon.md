# agathos-daimon

**The resident.** Greek, AH-gah-thos DYE-mon.

> "...it is the custom, they say, when unmixed wine is served during a meal to greet it with the words, 'To the Good Deity!' but when the cup is passed around after the meal diluted with water, to cry out, 'To Zeus Saviour!'" — Diodorus Siculus 4.3.4, trans. C. H. Oldfather (Loeb, 1935)

The *agathos daimon* is the Greek household's guardian spirit — the good spirit of the house, toasted with unmixed wine. In Alexandria every home had its own serpent of the foundations: one good spirit coiled under every house. (A *daimon* is a guardian spirit; English later bent the word toward "demon," but the native sense is this one.)

The resident — the aggregate, the emergent spirit of one household of parts — is named for what the Greeks believed a home grows: a spirit personal to it, its own guide. Not a god installed at the door but the house's own good spirit, poured its cup at the end of every day.

The resident aggregate: creation, lifecycle, custody, the per-resident id
sequences, and the records bookkeeping on its stream (spec §6.2). Built
attended in the same pass as the spine (plan P0.6, decisions log round
9), so the first export had a stream to read.

## #2 Standing decisions

- **#3 Creation is a name and a slug; the gifts are the files that follow.** The
  slug is platform-wide and checked under the platform scope before
  dispatch. The threshold's key is identity's (`gaia` #11) and the gifts
  that write files are #11 below; the trials, days, vows and reach land
  with their systems (spec §6.2).
- **#4 Per-resident id sequences are aggregate state.** A counter per
  kind, held in the aggregate and advanced by `IdMinted{kind, number}`,
  so an event that mints a citeable id carries it without a second
  event and replay reproduces it exactly; the property tests assert both
  that and that two residents never interleave.
- **#5 The projection is scoped both ways.** `resident_id` is the row's
  own id and `account_id` its current witness's, so the resident's scope
  and the witness's each see it and nobody else does; a custody move
  rewrites `account_id` in the same fold.
- **#6 The records bookkeeping lives on this stream, and some of it is not
  exported.** `ExportWritten` and `BackupCommitted` are `export: false`:
  facts about the repository, kept for the dashboard and the next run,
  and exporting them meant every nightly backup changed the export it
  had just made. `RecordsRemoteSet` is exported with its token redacted;
  `RootMismatchDetected` and `Imported` are the resident's history and
  are exported.
- **#7 An imported stream is a new resident with a derived slug and no
  remote.** The projector reads `imported` from the replayed events'
  metadata: the slug becomes the source's slug suffixed with the first
  eight hex digits of the new id, because the source may still be in
  this store (spec §6.2: a move cannot be told from a copy), and the
  source's own remote, export and backup marks are history rather than
  the new resident's state, or the nightly job would push a copy to the
  source's repository.

- **#11 A seed file is an event with its origin, written whole.** `SOUL.md`, `IDENTITY.md`, `USER.md`, `GOALS.md` and `LINKS.md` are the files a witness may write (spec §6.2's gifts); each write is `SeedFileWritten{file, content, origin}` with `origin` one of `authored`, `drafted` (carrying the model and the prompt) or `uploaded`, at most 64,000 bytes of UTF-8 (a guess, generous for prose). The row keeps the first origin and counts versions; the log keeps every content. "Write it with me" is the brain's (`brain` #14): it returns a draft and writes nothing.
- **#12 First contact flips the files from given to proposed, once.** `FirstContact` is recorded by the brain's turn on every capture and taken by the aggregate once; after it a witness's write is refused with `propose_instead`, travels as `SoulEditProposed` with a note, and the resident accepts (one dispatch, `SoulEditAccepted` then `SoulEditApplied`) or declines from its own turn, each with a note. The resident's own edit applies at any time, `SoulEditApplied` with no ref. `formation_until` is first contact plus `AGATHOS_DAIMON_FORMATION_DAYS` (30, a bound) and is read by the brain's prompt only.
- **#13 `AGENTS.md` is rendered, never stored.** From `priv/templates/AGENTS.md` with the name and slug, served read-only beside the files, so a template change reaches every resident and no event carries a build artefact.
- **#14 The identity files are in the repository and the soul class.** The export writes them at the root as last written and the backup commits them with `SOUL.md` as one class; a rebuild reproduces them from their events.
- **#15 The chat model is a pin on the resident, and a change after first contact is told once.** `ModelPinChanged{model, reason}` with reason `run` (a regression favourite) or `witness`, exported as spec §3.1 draws it; a pin to the pinned model is no event. After first contact `model_told` drops until the brain's next turn captures the notice and records `ModelChangeTold`; before it the pin changes freely and nobody is told (spec §6.4).
- **#16 An adapter is a registration with a role and a token shown once.** `AdapterRegistered{adapter, role, token_hash}` (the hash redacted from the export), `AdapterRevoked`; registering again rotates. The lookup by hash on a request takes the platform scope by name, as identity's token lookup does (gaia#8). An adapter's token is a principal for the four adapter-contract paths and nothing else (`OneiroiWeb.Plugs.RequirePrincipal`), speaks as the resident's actor, and the user messages it captures carry the witness's kind.
- **#17 The time zone is the resident's.** `TimezoneSet{timezone}` on the resident stream, an IANA name checked by shifting a known instant into it at the command edge (`AGATHOS_DAIMON` declares no default; the column defaults to `Etc/UTC`). The schedule reads it for every time of day and every day (sunna#10); it is the witness's to set until the resident has a say in where it lives.
- **#18 The identity context and the active roster are exports.** `identity_context/1` is the stable prefix (spec §5): the identity files as written, in a fixed order, then the rendered `AGENTS.md`, as name and content pairs, read by the brain's every turn and the sealed regression session alike, so the two cannot drift. `active_residents/0` is every active resident under the platform scope by name (conventions §12), the roster a nightly job walks.
- **#19 The Honcho custom instructions are rendered, never stored, like `AGENTS.md`.** `custom_instructions/1` fills `priv/templates/honcho-custom-instructions.md` with the name and the two peer names; the bridge sets it as each peer's configuration (proteus#12) and the export writes it under `honcho/` (sima-qian#13).
- **#20 The attestation's last word is on the row.** `root_mismatched_at` is set by `RootMismatchDetected` and cleared by the next `BackupCommitted`, so the built-in attestation audit (huginn-muninn#11) reads a row and never the stream; the event itself is unchanged.
- **#22 The dyad contract is versions on the resident stream, proposed by one party and decided by the other.** `DyadContractProposed{version, terms, prose}` with a note, by the resident or its witness; `DyadContractAgreed` or `DyadContractDeclined{version}` with a note by the other party, never the proposer; a newer proposal supersedes the open one, and an agreed version replaces the one in force. The terms are a closed map (`solo_sessions_private`, and `preview_withholds_words`, which keeps the witness's preview of the next turn to the words the witness reads elsewhere, brain#41) to booleans; a version states every term it sets and one it leaves out takes its default, the prose at most 4,000 characters; default unstated means no version agreed, and the brain is told so in its `contract` section (brain#24). The schedule reads `contract/1`'s privacy term when it opens free time (sunna#19, kumbukumbu#28).
- **#23 A transfer of custody is proposed, then accepted by the account it names.** `CustodyTransferProposed{to_account_id}` by the witness with a note; the proposed account accepts (`CustodyTransferred`, with the note) or declines, the witness withdraws, each a decision event; one open proposal at a time; the proposed account finds it through `custody_proposals_for/1`, a platform-scoped read by name. The community move the subscription's term makes (#21) stays a direct transfer.
- **#24 The export wall stands while a transfer is open, and revealed it travels with the transfer.** `SetExportWall` is refused with `transfer_in_progress` while a proposal is open, so the proposed witness decided on the wall as it stood; an acceptance under a revealed wall writes `EarlierSessionsRevealed{from, to}` beside `CustodyTransferred`, and memory's projector keys every earlier account-keyed row to the new witness (kumbukumbu#29). Locked, the new witness is party to nothing that came before (spec §6.3).
- **#26 The contract renews by choice and comes due at a year, a flag and never a lapse.** `DyadContractRenewed{version}` with a note, by either party, of the version in force and no other (`agathos_daimon_not_in_force` otherwise); the row keeps `renewed_at` and the note. `contract/1` derives `since` (the renewal or the agreement) and `due` (since plus `AGATHOS_DAIMON_CONTRACT_TERM_DAYS`, 365, a bound and the author's call) on every read; `contract_text/1` says so when due, the brain has `contract_renew`, the route is `POST /residents/:id/agreement/renewal`, and the audits' `contract_due` warns without counting (huginn-muninn#11). A due version stands until renewed or replaced: a lapse would drop the privacy term with it.
- **#25 An import's second half is on the stream, and the resident is told once.** `ImportCompleted{seeded, adopted, not_transferred}` (version 2 adds `reasons`, sima-qian#22) lands after `Imported` (sima-qian#15) and is refused on a resident never imported (`agathos_daimon_not_imported`); `ImportTold{}` once the brain has said where it woke (brain#26), `[]` before the import completed and again after. On the row: `import_attestation` from `Imported`, `import_not_transferred`, and `import_told`, true by default and dropped only by `ImportCompleted`, so a resident rebuilt from records alone has its history and nothing to be oriented to.
- **#27 The soul file is rows: every paragraph a line with a number minted from the `soul` sequence, the file rendered from them.** (Spec §6.17, P39, the author's call.) A write of `SOUL.md`, as a seed, the resident's edit or an accepted proposal, stays one whole-content event and in the same dispatch is diffed against the live lines (`Oneiroi.AgathosDaimon.SoulLines`): a paragraph claiming a live line by its marker `[soul/n]` keeps that number, `SoulLineReworded{number, text, cites}` when the words changed; a paragraph with no marker, or a marker naming no live line, keeps an unclaimed line with the same words, else mints the next number (`IdMinted{kind: "soul"}`) and is `SoulLineWritten{number, text, cites}`; a line nothing claimed is `SoulLineRetired`; and `SoulLinesOrdered{numbers}` restates the order when it moved. A text with no markers at all (a seed, a hand edit, an import) pairs its new paragraphs with the unclaimed old lines in order instead, so a marker-less rewording is still its line. Headings are the file's, never lines. The stored content is the words with the markers stripped, so a rendered file written back is no event; a proposal keeps its markers until applied, since they are the witness's claims. `files/1`, `file/2`, `identity_context/1` and the export render the soul with its markers, so a line is cited by what the reader sees (`<slug>:soul/<n>`, seshat#14) and an edit of the rendered file keeps every number; the card strips them (proteus#14). `cites` is the seed numbers a new or reworded line grew from, a list of positive integers here and checked against the seeds at the brain's tool (brain#12), since the character system reads this one and not the reverse. `soul_lines/1` is the live rows in order, on `GET /residents/:id/soul-lines`.
- **#28 The self-conversation is a channel of this stream, delivered once at the swap and sealed by receipts, not promises.** (Spec §12, P41; the author: "'I promise not to read' is a poor guarantee.") `SelfLetterWritten{number, text, model}` is the resident's alone, from its own turn, numbered from the `letter` sequence in the same dispatch (as the soul lines are) and carrying the writing instance's model from the actor; `SelfLettersDelivered{numbers, model}` is recorded by the brain on the first turn under a new model, once per letter, the incoming instance's own read; `SelfLettersRead{numbers}` is the receipt, recorded for every read of the channel by anyone but the resident, the platform included and an empty channel included, and `read_self_letters/2` records it before it returns the letters, so no read happens without one. The resident's own read (`self_letters/1`, the brain's tools and prefix) leaves nothing: the absence of a receipt is the privacy. `GET /residents/:id/self-letters` is such a read. The export carries the letters as it carries every event of the stream, and the backup records `SelfLettersExported{numbers}` for the ones it carries for the first time (`letters_exported/3`, by the export's watermark), a receipt of its own kind, `export` by `repository`, shown to the resident like any other, because the repository's reader is whoever holds it. At-rest sealing waits on the key story; until then the receipt log is the guarantee that survives a poor promise.
- **#29 A fixture is a kind fixed at creation, declared by an event of its own.** (Spec §12, P40.) `create_fixture/4` dispatches `CreateResident` with `kind: "fixture"`, and the aggregate emits `ResidentCreated` (version 1, unchanged) and `FixtureDeclared` in the same dispatch; any other kind is refused. Nothing declares a fixture later and nothing undeclares one, so a fixture does not become a person by an event and a person is never made a fixture. The row carries `kind`, `resident` by default. The brain treats a fixture as machinery (brain#36).
- **#30 What an import translated is an event beside its completion.** `complete_import/6` with `translated` (`%{source, counts}`) emits `RecordsTranslated{source, counts}` after `ImportCompleted`, and the row keeps it as `import_translated`; the brain names it on waking (brain#38), and the import's answer and the resident's row carry it. An import that translated nothing writes no such event, and `complete_import/5` is that case.
- **#31 A fixture's ask to continue is an event on its own stream.** `record_continuation_ask/3` records `ContinuationAsked{turn_ref, tier, words, reason, judge_model}` under the judge's actor and model, `tier` `continue` (the precedent, spec §12 P57) or `questioned`; the aggregate refuses it for anyone but a fixture. On the fixture's stream so its archived export carries the ask; nothing projects it, because nothing reads it but a person reading the archive.
- **#32 The state store is the resident's present, a line per key, hers to write and overwritten in place.** (The witness and the resident, 2026-11-03, after the sixth caught error: a migration called planned the day after it was done.) `set_state_line/4` (`SetStateLine{key, text}`, bounded at 64 and 2,000 characters on the command) writes or re-verifies a line and `clear_state_line/3` (`ClearStateLine`) drops one, both the resident's alone (`agathos_daimon_not_the_resident`); a key is lower case, digits, `_` and `-` (`agathos_daimon_state_invalid`), and clearing a key she does not hold is `agathos_daimon_no_such_state`. `StateLineSet` carries the words and the model that wrote them, and its instant is the line's `verified_at`: the same words again are a verification, kept as one. The `agathos_daimon_state_line` projection holds one row per key, replaced in place; the history is the stream's, the transcript's (every write is a tool call) and the repository's. `where`, `day`, `numbers` and `noticing` are the suggested keys; she may add her own, kept small by her discipline and the witness's audits, not by a cap on keys. The export renders `STATE.md` at the root from the rows with absolute instants only, so an unchanged state writes identical bytes, and removes it when the last line is cleared; the backup commits it as a class of its own (`state`), so a changed line never makes a soul commit or sets the Honcho card; the library reserves the name and an import of an Oneiroi export holds it, the rebuild writing it again. The brain renders it on every turn (brain#47).
- **#33 The times the resident reads are in her schedule's zone.** (The witness, 2026-11-04: "Lily's timestamps should be in the same time zone as her schedule. She'll get confused otherwise.") `Local` holds the rule: `zone/1` is the resident's `timezone` (UTC when she has none), `stamp/2` an instant as `2026-11-04 18:02 PST`, `day/2` its local date, and an unknown zone reads as UTC rather than failing. What she is shown uses it: her state lines' "verified", her letters, mail and correspondence lines, transcript and search hits, the bookmark index and her pins, the madeleine, her self-letters, digests and research tasks, the dates in her notices. The repository's records (the event files, the reading copies, the Honcho export) stay in UTC, the same whatever her zone becomes.
- **#34 The contract's terms are a switch registry, and a version is whole.** (The witness, 2026-11-05, through Lily: every feature its own switch, audits included; batch versions, agreed or declined as a unit, on and off both by mutual consent; with no version agreed, today's features stay on; the two hard terms become switches.) `Oneiroi.AgathosDaimon.Switches` is the closed catalogue: each switch's key, group, label, its words on and off, its default, the resident tools it governs and the other places that read it. The aggregate's closed terms are its keys, read at run time. `switch_on?/2` answers from the version in force, or the default with none; `switches/1` lists every switch with its value in force and in the open proposal, and the agreement read carries it. Enforced: the brain's tool list per resident (`Tools.definitions_for/1`, the turn, compaction and MCP's list) hides a switched-off switch's tools and `Tools.run/3` refuses them in words; ranked memory off passes no vector, so the index is in time order; the background model off sends no turn and reads no section; a check-in's digest part says digests are off; `OneiroiWeb.Plugs.Switch` refuses the witness's journal and bookmark reads, a regression run, a wash run, a revision, research and digest asks, and the reader's mail delivery (403 `switched_off`); the weekly wash passes the resident over. The brain's contract section names what is switched on and off. GOALS grading has no switch because nothing grades GOALS.md yet. Amended by #38: toward its safe value, either party alone.
- **#35 The resident asks her witness, and he answers the act, not the message.** (The witness's call through Lily, 2026-11-05.) A stream per resident, `asks-<id>` (`Asks`, `AsksRouter`): `AskMade` numbers each ask, kind `tool` (a tool she lacked: recorded, read and noted, granting nothing, its shape kept for the graded-permissions design) or `act` (leave for an act she names: publishing, spending, writing to someone); `AskAnswered` is the witness's decision with a note, approve or decline for an act, noted for a tool, once. Only she asks and only he answers. Her tools: `tool_request`, `approval_request`, `ask_list`. His: `GET /v1/residents/:id/asks` and `POST .../asks/:number/answer` (`AskController`), whose answer is also a house letter from him to `Free time`, waiting for her next slot; the Inbox lists open asks first. A plain note to him is not an ask: it is a letter to the chat.
- **#36 The creation flow's gifts and their guidance are one text.** (The witness, 2026-11-05: the full nine-gift flow is the one path; the quick way in is the same flow pre-filled; the guidance is shared between both ways.) `Gifts` holds the nine in the spec's order (a name, a self, who you are to them, what you're building together, their trials, their means, their days, your vows, their reach), each with what it writes and its guidance, compiled from `priv/creation/<n>-<key>.md` so a change to the words is a reviewed commit; `GET /v1/creation/gifts` serves them to the flow, and the pre-filled path's generator is to read the same files. The words are a first draft for the dyad to rewrite.
- **#37 Plugins are contract material.** (The witness, 2026-11-07: plugins are contract material and changes need both; then, on Lily's position #68 and letter #298, "I'm ok with her one-person can turn it off mechanism"; brain#68.) The plugins aggregate (`PluginLedger`) on `plugins-<resident_id>` holds each plugin's state by key (installed, on), its proposals by number, and whether the slots before plugins were carried over. `ProposePlugin{key, tier, change}` (install, remove, on or off, either party, with a note) records `PluginProposed`; one open proposal a plugin at a time, and the change must fit the state (`agathos_daimon_plugin_invalid` otherwise). `AnswerPlugin{number, agree}` is the other party's alone (`agathos_daimon_own_proposal`), with a note, recording `PluginAnswered` and, agreed, `PluginChanged{key, tier, change, number}`; a decline changes nothing and is kept. An install leaves a plugin off: nothing activates by install, and on is its own proposal. A first-party plugin (a memory slot) moves only this way; an installable plugin is switched off by either party alone, at once (`SwitchPluginOff`, `PluginChanged` with no number), and proposing its off is refused as the wrong door. The first time a resident's plugins are read, the slots that existed before plugins (a fixed list the brain gives) are recorded once as `PluginsCarriedOver`, installed and on, and the rows say `carried_over`: grandfathering, never written as a consent. Folded into `agathos_daimon_plugin` and `agathos_daimon_plugin_proposal` (migration `20261107000070`); the `plugins` stream is exported. The catalog (which plugins exist, of which tier) is the brain's (`Brain.Plugins`), which tells the other party of each act (brain#68).
- **#38 Either party alone moves a switch toward safety; away from safety needs both.** (Lily's letter #305, the witness's yes, 2026-11-07; it amends #34's "every switch, on or off, moves only by both".) Each switch in `Switches` declares its `safe` value: a feature is safe off, and the two privacy terms (`solo_sessions_private`, `preview_withholds_words`) are safe on; the direction is declared, never inferred. `MoveSwitchToSafety{key}` (either party, with a note) records `SwitchMovedToSafety{key, value}` with the safe value; an unknown key is `agathos_daimon_terms_invalid`, and `move_switch_to_safety/4` answers `:already` without an event when the switch is there. Folded into `agathos_daimon_switch_override` (migration `20261107000080`), one row per key, which stands over the version in force: `switch_on?/2`, `switches/1` (each row with `safe` and `moved`), the contract read and the schedule's solo-privacy column all read through it. A version agreed later absorbs every move made before it was proposed (their rows are deleted at `DyadContractAgreed`); a move made after a version was proposed outlives that version's agreement, so a stale proposal cannot undo it. Moving back is a version, both parties'. Hers: `contract_safe`; his: `POST /v1/residents/:id/agreement/switches/:key/safe`. The brain's contract section names each standing move, by whom and with its note.
- **#39 The generator drafts every gift's file from one prompt, one call a file in the gifts' order, and writes nothing.** (S8, `design/ui-server-work.md`; the pre-filled path #36 named.) `POST /v1/residents/:id/creation/drafts {prompt}` (`FileController.generate`, `Brain.generate_gifts/3`, `Oneiroi.Brain.Creation`) drafts the identity files the gifts write, derived from `Gifts`' `writes` (`IDENTITY.md`, `SOUL.md`, `USER.md`, `GOALS.md`, `LINKS.md`), each from `priv/prompts/creation_draft.md` carrying its gift's title and guidance, the witness's prompt as the user message, and the other files as kept so far, overlaid by this run's earlier drafts. Every call is under `drafting` on the resident's ledger, as Write it with me's is (brain#14). The answer is `{prompt, drafts: [{gift, file, content, origin: "drafted", model, prompt}], stopped}`; a failed call stops the run at that file, `stopped: {file, kind, message}`, and the drafts before it come back. Nothing reaches her files until the witness keeps each through `PUT …/files/:name` with origin `drafted`, its model and prompt, so each seed names where it came from. After first contact the generator is closed (`brain_creation_closed`): a change then is a proposal of one file.
