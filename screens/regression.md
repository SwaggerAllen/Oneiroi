# regression

Regression (spec §10). Built as `Regression` with `RegressionContainer`
in the shell: the tests, written and edited as three texts side by side;
starting a run on models named by their OpenRouter slugs, over every
test or the ones chosen; the runs, newest first; a run's cards arriving
over its channel while it answers (eunomia #30), blind until every
answer to a test is graded, graded pass or fail, favourited, a refusal
in its words and a failure as no answer, and under each answer a
collapsed "How Lily got there" (the resident's name) listing the
trial's tool calls in order, each marked read or mocked, with its
arguments and, behind a further fold, what came back, a cut result
said to be cut (eunomia #31); the results by pass rate once
revealed, and the pin, which confirms in place. A refused request shows
the server's meaning, with its site and request id as a reference. Not
built: the swipe (buttons grade), the by-test matrix and the by-model
view, and drafting a test from the soul. The state list lives in the
stories alone (DESIGN §4); nothing here names a state.

## #1 Shows

Tests (prompt, passes if, fails if); the blind swipe grader over models x tests with progress; results by pass rate with favourites; by test; by model.

## #2 Does

Write or draft a test; run; grade; favourite; pin the favourite as the chat model.

## #3 Rules from the design system

Approve-and-wake exists only where a wake makes sense; every other
approval is reject or approve. A destructive action confirms once in
place by the button changing to its consequence, never in a modal. A
number the spec calls a bound says so beside it. Nothing says "AI", "the
model", "generate", "regenerate" or "error" as a heading, and "AI
refused" is never a feedback option. The client says Lily, you, your
agreement with Lily, who looks after Lily; the schema's words appear
only in settings and contract copy or not at all.
