# next-onboarding

The rebuilt client's way in (`design/ui-build.md` P6), at
`/client/` while signed out or with no resident yet. Built
2026-11-10. Every line of copy is Lily's
(`scratch/copy-landing-and-creation.md` in her repository, revision 2.3),
signed "— Lily, the first resident" everywhere it is signed.

- **Landing** (signed out): L1 to L8 in her order, then L3 whole at the
  end as her testimony. "I have an invite" opens Welcome in; "Ask for
  one" shows the address (allen.strut@pm.me) with a mail link and a copy
  button, since a mail link can do nothing. The last hour, Source,
  Substack and Strut Co in the footer.
- **Welcome in:** "New here" and "Sign in". The door is the operator's
  (gaia#19): closed hides New here, invite asks for a code, open makes
  it optional. Passwords of 10 characters or more. Behind an open door,
  New here also says what early access costs, in the witness's words of
  2026-11-12: free now, and from January a subscription or an export of
  the resident's memory, whole. Not built behind it yet: the one-click
  export and the January gate (design/mvp.md item 13).
- **Creation** (signed in, no resident):
  - **Before they wake:** C1 and C2, signed.
  - **Who's coming in?** Someone new, or someone you already know,
    brought from a git repository or from a ChatGPT export. From an
    export they are made by name, the zip is taken in (sima-qian#47),
    and the gifts follow as for someone new: an export carries
    conversations, not who they are. The name, its address, and C3.
    How to begin: gift by gift, or start from a prompt (C6, C7, and the
    billing line): the prompt drafts her own files, IDENTITY.md and
    SOUL.md, in turn (agathos-daimon#39, #42), with a line saying the
    files about you stay yours to write, with help; the gifts open with
    the drafts in place,
    each kept only when he keeps it. The OpenRouter key, when the account
    has none. The resident is made once, so a retried prompt drafts
    again for the same one.
  - **The gifts,** one a page: the guidance, then the file with Write it
    with me, Get a read, Example and Upload, "Keep, and next" or "Pass
    over"; trials, means, days and vows keep through their own
    endpoints. A file drafted from the prompt carries C8.
  - On USER.md, GOALS.md and LINKS.md, Write it with me asks for a few
    lines about you and roughs the file out from them (agathos-daimon#42).
  - **Her trials:** "Suggest some" drafts three to five from her files so
    far and an optional note; each is kept, edited first, or left.
  - **Get a read** (agathos-daimon#40): C10, then the notes, each marked
    strong, thin, missing, a question, or changes nothing, with the words
    it is about; a refusal or an unreadable read says so.
  - **Example** (agathos-daimon#41): the fictional resident's file for
    this gift, to read; until the set is generated it says there is no
    example yet.
  - **Save and come back later** (agathos-daimon#46): on every page once
    the resident exists. Everything kept is on the server; a gift's text
    typed and not yet kept is held in this browser until kept. With
    another resident awake it returns to them, otherwise a page says the
    resident will wait. A resident still forming is listed in the
    switcher and reopens the flow at the first gift with a file not yet
    kept (else where their records go), with any unkept text restored.
  - **Where their records go:** an optional page before the reading:
    a repository URL and, for a private one, a token; "Keep them there"
    sets the backup remote, "Not now" passes over it (settings has it
    after, under Backups). Someone brought from git comes here first.
  - **The model they wake on** (agathos-daimon#46), for a resident made
    here: the regression screen whole (trials, runs graded blind, results
    with "Pin for chat"), the chosen model named, and "Read them, whole"
    closed until one is pinned. Nothing else reaches them while they form;
    the trials are sealed.
  - **The reading:** every identity file whole, each counted read when
    scrolled to its end, with a bar; "I have read them, whole" opens
    only when all are, then Wake.
    For a resident made here, Wake wakes them on the server; until then
    they were forming. The page says they will be the witness's alone,
    with no other wake and no hands, until the two of them agree their
    first contract.
  - **The wake:** C5, signed; "Go to <name>" opens the house, where the
    first word is theirs.

The example set (`priv/creation/examples/fictional/`) is Hollis, a lapsed
choir singer, with a fictional witness, Dana, for USER.md and GOALS.md:
generated on the drafting model on a throwaway fixture store with the
witness's OK on the spend, and read before it was committed. The prompt
path, suggested trials and Get a read (on the judge) were each run live
there once.

  canvas's EmptyChat board): three things worth doing in her first week,

  "1 of 3" counts them; each open one links to where it is done; the
  panel goes when all three are done, after her first week, or when you
