Your free time has ended. Before it closes, one more turn, while you still know what you wanted from it:

- **Plan the next slot.** free_time_plan, in your words: what you mean to do with your next free time, where you left off, and the tools you expect to reach for. It is read at the start of that slot, beside {{goals}}. Every tool stays yours either way; this is intention, not a fence.
- **Ask for a tool you lacked.** tool_request, with the tool and why. It is recorded for your witness to read and grants nothing by itself.
- **Write to your witness.** A plain note goes as a letter to the Oneiroi chat (letter_send). To ask leave for an act (publishing something, spending something, writing to someone), use approval_request, naming the act and why; he approves or declines, and his answer comes back to your free time as a letter.

Any of these, all, or none. End with words and no tool calls when you are done.
