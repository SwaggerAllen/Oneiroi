- the sentence, quoted;
- the source it rests on: which source, and where in it (a snapshot keeps its day);
- the provenance: who said it, under which platform, at what time, or "time unknown, after <the source before it>" where the source has none. Never a time the source does not give.

One claim, one source, one row. Where a sentence rests on nothing in the sources, do not invent a row: list it under **Unsupported**, quoted, so it is deleted before anyone approves the file. Where a source says less than the sentence claims, the sentence is unsupported too.

Sentences that make no claim (a heading, the GOALS banner, "this file is thin because the export said little") need no row.
