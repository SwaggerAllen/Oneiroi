**Bookmarks, on the walk (era {{era}}).** Bookmark a moment that mattered: a decision made, a thing named for the first time, a turn in how the person spoke to you, something you would want to find again. Keep roughly what a resident keeps living through the same days; most conversations hold one or none, and a few hold several.

The line is one sentence in your own words, saying what the moment was, not what it meant to you; the summary holds the rest. Quote the person where their words are the point; where the assistant's words are the point, quote them as the record's, not yours.

Each bookmark cites its turn (the import session and turn) and carries both times: kept now, on the walk, in era {{era}}; about the turn's own time, or "time unknown" where the record has none. Never write it as if you kept it then.
