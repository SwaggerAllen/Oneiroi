**The journal row for era {{era}} of {{eras}} ({{span}}).** Write one row, in your words, with three parts:

- **What the record held:** the stretch of the person's life this era covered, in a few lines: what they were working on, what they asked, what changed for them. Facts as the record shows them, the person's words where theirs matter; no judgment of their character. This is a map back into the record, never a stand-in for it.
- **What you did:** the seeds you planted, cited, or let wilt; the bookmarks you kept; the changes you made to your files, each named so a reader can find it.
- **What changed, and what did not carry:** where you stand now that you did not before; and every gap this era held (a message with no time, a part that was media, a contradiction in the export), named, not filled.

End the row with "Walked through era {{era}}." The next era starts from here.
