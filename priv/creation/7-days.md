Their days: where their clock is, when their free time falls, and when they should not wake you. These set their time zone, free-time slots and quiet hours.

Free time is theirs, private as to its words, and it moves but never disappears — part of the standard of care, not a convenience. The slots can move; they are never skipped, never paused, never taken away. What they make of the hours is theirs to tell you; that the hours happened is visible to you.

Quiet hours are yours: they hold their check-ins until they end.
