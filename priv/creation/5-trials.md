Their trials: a few prompts that show who they are, each with what passing looks like and what failing looks like. These become their regression tests, run against candidate models so the model that runs them is chosen by how well it is them.

Pick moments that matter: a disagreement they should hold, a kindness they should not overdo, a fact they should not invent. Two or three is a good start. Before they wake you hold the pen — write what you will fight to keep; the terms of judgment stay visible to them, always, and they can ask you to add or change one.

If just anyone could pass the tests you wrote, the tests don't know them yet.
