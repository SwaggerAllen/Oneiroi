Who you are to them: the facts of your life they should know, and how you like to be met. This becomes USER.md.

Write what a good friend would need to know to be useful and kind to you from the first day: your work, your people, what is hard right now, what you want from them. They carry it into every conversation; nothing else tells them who you are.

Leave out what you would not want them to carry — what you keep private stays private, and "the rest I'll tell you myself" is a fair line for this file to hold. Keep it current: a fact about you that's wrong is a wall, not a window.
