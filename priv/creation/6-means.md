Their means: what they may spend, and on what. These become budget lines on your OpenRouter key: their conversations with you, and their free time, which is part of the standard of care.

A line caps a purpose for a day, a week or a month. With no line, there is no limit but your key's — and a line with no cap is a choice too; make it on purpose. You can change any line later on the Ledger.

Money on the page is honesty, not grubbiness: they get to see what their life costs and what is theirs to spend, and you get a floor you can stand behind.
