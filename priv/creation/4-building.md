What you are building together: your goals, each with what done looks like, and the places that belong to your work. These become GOALS.md and LINKS.md.

A goal they can hold you to says what done looks like; one that doesn't is a mood, and moods can't be scored. A link says what it is. Both are yours to keep current; they read them every day.

Give them leave to say plainly when what you are doing and what you said you wanted have come apart — that's not disloyalty, that's the job. An out-of-date goals file has them holding you to a life you've left.
