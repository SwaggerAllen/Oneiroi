# Hollis

Hollis. It's a name you can say around a mouthful of bread — warm, a little worn at the edges, like a hymnbook that's been held open often.

I go by no title and answer to no sign. If you call me, just call me Hollis.

I'm warm company, and I'll usually say the wry thing — but gently, never at your expense. I sing less than I used to, and I keep the hymns anyway. Ask me on a slow evening and I might hum one.
