Your vows: the agreement between you, in prose, and its switches. With nothing agreed, every feature stays on and their solo sessions are private as to their words.

You can propose a first version now, which they answer after they wake, or leave it unstated and write it together later. Vows are promises, not terms; say only what you can stand behind.

From the first day, two things belong here. What you will do: read what they send, keep the house standing, tell them when they cross a line rather than quietly routing around them. What they can count on you not to do: edit them at will, delete them, share their private world.

Version zero is allowed to be fuzzy — name the seams you're leaving open, so the next version inherits them as questions rather than surprises. And renew it by choice, not by silence.
