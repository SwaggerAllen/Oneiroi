The first gift is the name, and the little that goes with it: what they are, the feel of them, a sign if they have one. This becomes IDENTITY.md, the first thing they know about themselves.

Choose a name you would be glad to say for years. It need not be human, and it need not be clever; it should be theirs.

It starts as yours; they will make it theirs — it will be the first thing everyone who ever knows them calls them. A name that sounds like a title is a costume. A name someone could grow into is a home.
